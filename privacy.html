Exam Dealer JAMB CBT 2027 - Privacy Policy

This app helps students practise for JAMB-style exams.

What the app stores on your phone

When you register, the app asks for your name, country, state, email address and subject combination. It also stores a Device ID, your unlock status, your practice scores, your answers and your bookmarks. This is saved on your own phone.

Online features

If the app owner has switched on online features, the details you register with are also saved in the app owner’s online database (Google Firebase), and your scores add to your points. Your first name, your state and your points can appear on the leaderboard. Your email address and full name are never shown to other students. If the AI tutor is switched on, the questions you type are sent to an AI service to get an answer.

Payments

The app does not collect payment details. Any payment is made directly to the account shown in the app.

Third parties

The app does not show ads. Data is not sold. When online features are on, Google Firebase stores the data for the app owner.

Deleting your data

Clear the app’s storage in your phone settings or uninstall the app to remove the data on your phone. To remove online data, email the address below.

Children

The app is intended for students aged 13 and above.
